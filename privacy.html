Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 26, 2023

At Dinner Menu Recommendation, we take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how we collect and use your data.

1. Data Collection

We do not require user accounts. However, we collect data through the following third-party services:

  • Formspree: If you use our contact form, your email and message are sent through Formspree.
  • Disqus: If you comment, Disqus may collect data as outlined in their privacy policy.
  • Google AdSense: Google uses cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to our website or other websites.

2. Google AdSense & Cookies

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Ads Settings.

3. Data Usage

We only use the data provided via our contact form to respond to your inquiries. We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Third-Party Links

Our site may contain links to other websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our Contact Page.
